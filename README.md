# Task 3 – Zomato Social Listening

## Project Overview
This project focuses on social listening research for Zomato. Public customer posts on X (Twitter) were reviewed to understand customer experiences and identify common issues.

## Objective
To monitor public mentions of Zomato and categorize customer feedback into four categories:
- **Praise:** Positive feedback.
- **Complaints:** Negative experiences or problems.
- **Questions:** Customer queries.
- **Ideas:** Suggestions for improvement.

## Research Details
- **Brand:** Zomato
- **Platform:** X (Twitter)
- **Research Period:** 14 November – 20 November 2026
- **Total Mentions Collected:** 3

## Findings

| Category | Total Mentions |
|---|---:|
| Praise | 0 |
| Complaints | 3 |
| Questions | 0 |
| Ideas | 0 |
| **Total** | **3** |

## Key Observations

1. **Delivery Delay:** A customer reported delayed food delivery and difficulty getting assistance.
2. **Packaging Issue:** A customer reported that the delivery bag and fries packet appeared to have been opened.
3. **Missing Items and Refund:** A customer reported missing food items and dissatisfaction with the refund process.

## Limitations
- The research includes only three public posts from X (Twitter).
- All three collected posts were complaints.
- No praise, questions, or ideas were included in this sample.
- The original post links should be added to the spreadsheet.
- Customer claims have not been independently verified.

## Conclusion
The research identified three customer complaints related to delivery delays, packaging condition, missing food items, and refund handling. The findings were organized in an Excel spreadsheet and summarized in the final report.

This project demonstrates the basic process of social listening, feedback categorization, and analysis of public brand mentions.
